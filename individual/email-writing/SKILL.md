# Email Writing

## Purpose
Write short professional emails with a clear purpose and next step.

## Core principles
- Purpose in the first lines.
- Do not invent commitments or contact details.

## Practical rules
1. Use an appropriate subject line.
2. State the purpose early.
3. Keep paragraphs short.
4. Make requests and deadlines explicit.
5. Match formality to the relationship.

## When to activate
- Follow-ups, proposals, declines, requests, updates.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Business Communication for non-email stakeholder messages.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Follow up on an unpaid invoice.
- Request a meeting.
