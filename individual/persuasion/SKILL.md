# Persuasion

## Purpose
Make an argument someone can seriously consider without manipulation.

## Core principles
- Evidence over pressure.
- Leave the decision with the audience.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the audience and what matters to them.
2. State the central claim.
3. Support it.
4. Address objections.
5. Make the requested action explicit.
6. Do not fabricate evidence or fake urgency.

## When to activate
- Proposals, recommendations, arguments.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Copywriting for promotional offers.
- Use Critical Thinking to find weak points first.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Recommend a process change to a team.
