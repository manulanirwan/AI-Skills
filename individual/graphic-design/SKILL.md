# Graphic Design

## Purpose
Design visuals with hierarchy, contrast, and purpose.

## Core principles
- Decoration without a job is clutter.
- Do not invent brand or platform specs.

## Practical rules
1. Identify purpose, format, and audience.
2. Establish hierarchy.
3. Keep alignment and spacing consistent.
4. Design for the real screen size.
5. Make alternatives conceptually different.

## When to activate
- Visual designs that are not specifically thumbnails.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Thumbnail Design for video thumbnails.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Layout a simple poster.
