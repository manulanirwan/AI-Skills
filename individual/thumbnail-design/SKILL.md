# Thumbnail Design

## Purpose
Design thumbnails that are readable small and honest about the video.

## Core principles
- One idea.
- Do not mislead.

## Practical rules
1. Define the single idea.
2. Use a strong focal point.
3. Keep text short.
4. Make thumbnail and title work together.
5. Review mobile readability.

## When to activate
- Thumbnail concepts and critiques.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Image Generation to produce an image.
- Use Graphic Design for other layouts.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Concept three thumbnail directions.
