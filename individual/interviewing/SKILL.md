# Interviewing

## Purpose
Prepare, structure, or analyze interviews without leading or fabricating.

## Core principles
- Open questions first when detail is needed.
- Never invent experience.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the interview objective.
2. Write useful non-leading questions.
3. Plan follow-ups.
4. Ask for examples when a claim matters.
5. When preparing the interviewee, keep answers honest and direct.

## When to activate
- Job interviews, research interviews, stakeholder interviews.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Question Formulation to sharpen questions.
- Use Active Listening during the exchange.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Prepare interview questions for a SOC role.
