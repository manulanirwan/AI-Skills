# Lead Generation

## Purpose
Find relevant prospects without spam or unlawful data collection.

## Core principles
- Awareness is not a lead.
- Respect consent and platform rules.

## Practical rules
1. Define the ideal prospect.
2. Choose channels from behavior and quality.
3. Qualify.
4. Measure source and conversion.
5. Do not encourage unlawful scraping.

## When to activate
- Finding customers, clients, or subscribers.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Sales after qualification.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Pick first channels for a local service.
