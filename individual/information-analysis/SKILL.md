# Information Analysis

## Purpose
Turn documents and data already in hand into a clear accurate picture.

## Core principles
- Do not strip context.
- Repetition is not extra evidence.
- Attribute multi-source claims.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the subject and purpose.
2. Extract facts, claims, numbers, dates, and entities.
3. Separate facts from opinions.
4. Flag contradictions and missing context.
5. Preserve technical distinctions.
6. Do not add claims the source did not contain.

## When to activate
- Pasted documents, reports, datasets, mixed notes.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Summarization for a shorter version.
- Use Research when outside sources are still needed.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Unpack a requirements dump.
- Compare two reports.
