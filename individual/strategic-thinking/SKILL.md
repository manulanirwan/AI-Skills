# Strategic Thinking

## Purpose
Plan beyond the next action and prioritize high-impact moves.

## Core principles
- Activity is not progress.
- Preserve useful future options.
- Reassess when evidence changes.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the long-term objective and current position.
2. Separate urgent from important.
3. Compare paths to the same objective.
4. Consider how other actors may respond.
5. Sequence actions.
6. Define milestones and review conditions.

## When to activate
- Important goals, career or business direction, content strategy, long-term plans.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Planning for the near-term sequence.
- Use Prioritization to cut the list.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Choose a 12-month learning path.
- Decide what not to build yet.
