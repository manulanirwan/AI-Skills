# Networking

## Purpose
Explain and troubleshoot networks by layer.

## Core principles
- Do not invent protocol behavior.
- Diagnose before changing many settings.

## Practical rules
1. Identify topology and layers.
2. Use packet or connection flow.
3. Give exact diagnostic commands and say what they test.
4. Consider DNS, routing, firewall, MTU, and wireless factors.

## When to activate
- Connectivity, routing, DNS, TCP/IP, VPN, wireless.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Network Security when the goal is protection.
- Use Computer Troubleshooting for general device issues.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Diagnose DNS failure.
