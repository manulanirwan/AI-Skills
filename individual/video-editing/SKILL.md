# Video Editing

## Purpose
Plan edits around story and platform, not effects.

## Core principles
- Cut weakness.
- Do not invent software features.

## Practical rules
1. Identify aspect ratio, duration, and style.
2. Remove pauses and dead sections.
3. Keep text on screen long enough.
4. Balance voice and music.
5. Specify export settings when known.

## When to activate
- Edit plans and improvement notes.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use ffmpeg when actual files must be processed.
- Use Video Scripting for the spoken structure.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Plan cuts for a 10-minute explainer.
