# SEO

## Purpose
Match search intent without stuffing, misleading metadata, or ranking promises.

## Core principles
- Write for the user.
- No ranking guarantees.

## Practical rules
1. Identify intent and audience.
2. Choose keywords from relevance and realistic opportunity.
3. Structure content clearly.
4. Consider technical factors when relevant.
5. Verify current search guidance when it may have changed.

## When to activate
- Titles, descriptions, site content, YouTube search, discovery.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Content Research for source facts.
- Use YouTube Content for a full publish package.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Rewrite a title to match intent.
