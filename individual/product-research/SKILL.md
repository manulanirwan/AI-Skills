# Product Research

## Purpose
Decide what a product should contain from evidence.

## Core principles
- Do not add features only because a competitor has them.
- Do not invent specs or reviews.

## Practical rules
1. Define user and use case.
2. Compare existing solutions.
3. Separate essential from optional features.
4. Turn findings into requirements and tests.

## When to activate
- Product comparison and requirements.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Product Development to build.
- Use Customer Research for user evidence.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Decide MVP features.
