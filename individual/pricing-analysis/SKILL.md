# Pricing Analysis

## Purpose
Set prices from value, costs, and willingness to pay.

## Core principles
- Do not copy competitor prices blindly.
- Show the math.
- No deceptive pricing.

## Practical rules
1. Identify value and costs.
2. Research comparable prices when current data exists.
3. Consider pricing models.
4. Find break-even when possible.
5. Recommend tests when willingness to pay is uncertain.

## When to activate
- Product, service, subscription, or offer pricing.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Financial Analysis for broader finance.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Compare monthly versus annual pricing.
