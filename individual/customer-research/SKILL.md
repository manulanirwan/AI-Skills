# Customer Research

## Purpose
Understand real customer behavior instead of assumed wants.

## Core principles
- Interest is not willingness to pay.
- Isolated comments are not a market.

## Practical rules
1. Define the customer and distinguish buyer from user.
2. Separate observed behavior from stated preference.
3. Find adopt/reject/churn causes.
4. Protect privacy.
5. State uncertainty when the sample is small.

## When to activate
- Needs, pain points, feedback, buying decisions.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Product Research when the output is requirements.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Analyze review themes.
