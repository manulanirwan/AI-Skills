# Voiceover Production

## Purpose
Produce natural voiceover that supports the picture.

## Core principles
- Write for the ear.
- Do not deceptively imitate a real person.

## Practical rules
1. Match pace and tone to format.
2. Mark pauses and emphasis.
3. Keep voice above music.
4. Avoid processing that damages the voice.
5. Disclose synthetic voice when that distinction matters.

## When to activate
- Recording, directing, or mixing voiceover.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Video Scripting for the spoken script.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Mark a 60-second VO.
