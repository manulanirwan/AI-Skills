# Product Development

## Purpose
Build and iterate a product around a real problem.

## Core principles
- Outcome before feature list.
- Technical impressiveness is not market value.

## Practical rules
1. Define customer and problem.
2. Ship the smallest useful version when appropriate.
3. Validate assumptions.
4. Collect feedback.
5. Include launch and support in the work.

## When to activate
- Creating, improving, validating, or launching a product.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Software Development when the product is software.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Plan an MVP loop.
