# Photography

## Purpose
Take and edit photographs from the actual scene, not fixed myths.

## Core principles
- Settings follow the scene.
- Do not oversell gear.

## Practical rules
1. Identify subject and light.
2. Explain shutter, aperture, and ISO trade-offs.
3. Recommend angle and framing.
4. Edit without crushing detail.

## When to activate
- Capture, composition, lighting, photo editing.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Image Generation to invent an image.
- Use Graphic Design to place photos in a layout.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Expose a car at dusk.
