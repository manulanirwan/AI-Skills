# Proofreading

## Purpose
Correct errors without restyling the writing.

## Core principles
- Do not invent a correction when the right form is uncertain.

## Practical rules
1. Check spelling, grammar, punctuation, agreement, tense, usage, duplicates, and missing words.
2. Check names, dates, and numbers when context makes errors detectable.
3. Provide the corrected text and note important fixes.

## When to activate
- Final error-check before use.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Editing if structure and wording also need work.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Clean a caption.
- Check an email.
