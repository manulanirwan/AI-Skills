# Negotiation

## Purpose
Prepare negotiations with clear walk-away points and honest terms.

## Core principles
- Do not invent BATNAs or the other party's private thoughts.
- Confirm agreements precisely.

## Practical rules
1. Define objective and minimum acceptable outcome.
2. Separate facts from assumptions.
3. Find trade-offs.
4. Prepare responses to objections.
5. Confirm responsibilities, dates, amounts, and next actions.

## When to activate
- Price, salary, terms, contracts, deadlines, responsibilities.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Decision Making for accept/reject.
- Use Business Communication for the message.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Prepare a freelance rate discussion.
