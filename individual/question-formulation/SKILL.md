# Question Formulation

## Purpose
Rewrite questions so they can actually be answered.

## Core principles
- Neutral when neutrality matters.
- Do not smuggle the desired answer into the wording.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the real information needed.
2. Make the question specific.
3. Split mixed questions.
4. Flag missing context.
5. Distinguish fact, opinion, evaluation, prediction, and decision questions.

## When to activate
- Vague questions, research questions, investigation framing.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Research after the question is sharp.
- Use Interviewing for live question sequences.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Turn 'is this good?' into a measurable question.
