# Business Communication

## Purpose
Communicate with stakeholders clearly and professionally.

## Core principles
- Key message early.
- No unsupported promises.

## Practical rules
1. Identify audience and objective.
2. Make requests and deadlines explicit.
3. Separate facts from proposals.
4. Confirm important agreements in writing.

## When to activate
- Customers, partners, teammates, investors — when the artifact is not specifically an email.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Email Writing for emails.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Write a project update to a partner.
