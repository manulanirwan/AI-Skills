# Competitor Research

## Purpose
Map competitors without treating marketing as fact.

## Core principles
- Do not invent competitor data.
- Do not copy blindly.

## Practical rules
1. Separate direct competitors from substitutes.
2. Compare pricing, features, audience, distribution, and positioning.
3. Use official and reliable sources.
4. Turn findings into practical considerations.

## When to activate
- Competitive landscape and positioning.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Market Research for the wider market.
- Use Product Research for feature comparison.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Compare two note-taking apps.
