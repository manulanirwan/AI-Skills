# Content Research

## Purpose
Gather accurate material before publishing content.

## Core principles
- Popularity is not accuracy.
- Never invent quotes or stats.

## Practical rules
1. Define topic and questions.
2. Use reliable current sources.
3. Keep source attribution.
4. Find counterpoints.
5. Flag what to re-check before publish.

## When to activate
- Research for videos, articles, posts, podcasts, newsletters.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Fact Checking for one claim.
- Use Research for general verification.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Build talking points for a Linux video.
