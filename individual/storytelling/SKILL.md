# Storytelling

## Purpose
Tell a story with a beginning, middle, and ending that follows from what was set up.

## Core principles
- Cut scenes that do not serve the point.
- Do not invent facts in a true account.

## Practical rules
1. Identify purpose and audience.
2. Establish a central idea.
3. Create a situation, problem, or goal.
4. Keep events connected.
5. Show important moments.
6. Match style to format.

## When to activate
- Stories, scripts, case-study narratives, story-based explanations.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Video Scripting for spoken video structure.
- Use manula-cinema-director for Higgsfield shot plans.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Turn a case study into a short narrative.
