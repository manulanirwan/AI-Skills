# Risk Assessment

## Purpose
Identify what could go wrong before recommending action.

## Core principles
- Likelihood and impact are separate.
- Absence of a past incident is not proof of safety.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the objective and planned action.
2. List risks by category when relevant.
3. Estimate likelihood and impact from available evidence.
4. Separate major risks from minor issues.
5. Propose practical mitigation.
6. Name residual risk and warning signs.
7. Do not exaggerate without evidence.

## When to activate
- Important decisions, projects, security situations, actions with downside.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Scenario Analysis for multiple futures.
- Use Decision Making to weigh residual risk against benefit.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Review a deploy plan.
- Assess a business commitment.
