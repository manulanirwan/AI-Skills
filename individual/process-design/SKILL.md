# Process Design

## Purpose
Design repeatable operational processes that are no more complex than needed.

## Core principles
- Automate after the process is understood.

## Practical rules
1. Map trigger, steps, owners, decisions, and output.
2. Cut duplicate work and bottlenecks.
3. Separate exceptions.
4. Define metrics.
5. Test with realistic scenarios.

## When to activate
- Standardizing, documenting, or improving a workflow.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Automation to implement unattended steps.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Design an intake-to-publish process.
