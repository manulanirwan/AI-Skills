# Business Planning

## Purpose
Turn an idea into a plan grounded in customers, economics, and validation.

## Core principles
- Validate cheaply before spending.
- Do not invent revenue.

## Practical rules
1. Define customer, problem, offer, and model.
2. Identify costs, risks, and assumptions.
3. Define milestones.
4. Keep the plan inside available resources.

## When to activate
- Venture ideas, product or service businesses, online businesses.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Financial Analysis and Pricing Analysis for numbers.
- Use aiyubovan-ikigai when the offer is still unknown.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Draft an MVP plan.
