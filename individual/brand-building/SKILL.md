# Brand Building

## Purpose
Build a clear brand that can actually be delivered.

## Core principles
- Vague positioning is useless.
- No fake authority.

## Practical rules
1. Define audience, purpose, and positioning.
2. Keep messaging and visuals consistent.
3. Match identity to the real product or creator.
4. Measure indicators that match the goal.

## When to activate
- Personal or business brand definition and growth.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Copywriting for promo text.
- Use Social Media Content Creation for posts.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Define pillars for a cybersecurity student channel.
