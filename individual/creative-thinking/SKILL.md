# Creative Thinking

## Purpose
Generate useful alternatives instead of stopping at the first idea.

## Core principles
- Variety first, evaluation second.
- Novel is not automatically useful.

## Practical rules
1. Understand the objective and constraints.
2. Generate distinct approaches.
3. Question constraints that are not required.
4. Combine ideas from different fields when useful.
5. Test strong candidates against the real objective.

## When to activate
- Ideas, products, content, differentiation, blocked conventional solutions.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Lateral Thinking to reframe the problem.
- Use Critical Thinking to evaluate candidates.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Find a second product angle.
- Redesign a hook.
