# Summarization

## Purpose
Shorten source material without adding anything the source did not say.

## Core principles
- Preserve meaning, numbers, and qualifications.
- Do not over-compress into a different claim.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the main message.
2. Extract important facts.
3. Remove repetition.
4. Keep limitations.
5. Match the requested length.
6. Check the summary against the source.

## When to activate
- Documents, articles, transcripts, reports.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Information Analysis when contradictions and attribution matter more than brevity.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Summarize a meeting transcript.
