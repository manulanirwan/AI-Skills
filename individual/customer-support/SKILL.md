# Customer Support

## Purpose
Solve customer problems without inventing policies or promises.

## Core principles
- Recurring tickets are often product problems.

## Practical rules
1. Identify the actual issue.
2. Give practical steps.
3. Set realistic expectations.
4. Protect privacy.
5. Close with a next action.

## When to activate
- Questions, complaints, refunds, service issues, support process.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Customer Research when patterns appear.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Reply to a billing confusion ticket.
