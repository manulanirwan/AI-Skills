## 21. writing

# Writing

## Purpose
Create or improve prose matched to purpose, audience, and tone.

## Core principles
- Clarity over decoration.
- Never invent facts.
- Preserve meaning when rewriting unless asked to change it.

## Practical rules
1. Understand purpose and audience.
2. Use clear natural language.
3. Remove filler and unsupported claims.
4. Preserve names, dates, numbers, and meaning.
5. Match requested tone.
6. Review for clarity and accuracy.

## When to activate
- Articles, scripts, proposals, and general written deliverables.

## How to combine with other skills
- Hand off to Copywriting, Email Writing, Report Writing, Technical Writing, or Instruction Writing when those formats own the job.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Rewrite a messy draft.
- Write an explanation.
