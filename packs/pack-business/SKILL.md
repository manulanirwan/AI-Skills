## 76. market-research

# Market Research

## Purpose
Determine whether a market exists and how it behaves.

## Core principles
- A large audience is not a profitable market.
- Social attention is not demand.

## Practical rules
1. Define the market, geography, customer group, and period.
2. Separate facts from estimates.
3. Identify competitors and barriers.
4. State what evidence would validate demand.
5. Do not invent market size.

## When to activate
- Markets, demand, industry trends, commercial opportunity.

## How to combine with other skills
- Use Customer Research and Competitor Research for those slices.

## Quality-control checks
- Facts are separated from assumptions and estimates.
- Nothing important was invented.
- The output matches the user's actual objective.
- A practical next action is clear when action is needed.

## Example applications
- Assess demand for a study-notes product.
